What the labels mean

New means found in the latest daily run. Area: ML, SWE, research, data, quant, hardware or other. When: Dec–Feb (Brazilian summer break), Jul (July break), May–Aug (northern summer), year-round (during the semester) or unknown. Hover over it to see how it fits UNESP's semesters (Mar–Jul, Aug–Dec).

Warnings are hints, not verdicts: US auth? may need US work authorization, PhD? may target PhD students, grad year? mentions a graduation-year limit, new grad is a job for after graduation, unsure may not be a student role.
