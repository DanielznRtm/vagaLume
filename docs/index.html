How to read this page

Fit compares each role with your profile: ● good fit, ◐ check something (a visa, a graduation-year rule, a job for after you graduate), ○ long shot (for example a PhD-only role while you're an undergrad). Open a row to see why.

When UNESP semester (Mar–Jun, Aug–Nov) break (Dec–Feb, Jul) role runs in a break role runs during the semester

Pay is shown when the posting states it (hr = per hour, mo = per month, yr = per year). Annual figures for internships are usually the hourly rate scaled to a year.

The bot keeps every student posting it finds. Labels and fit are hints, not verdicts. Applied, Hide and your profile are saved only in this browser. For statuses every device sees, use config/status.yaml in the repository.
