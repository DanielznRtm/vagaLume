How to read this page

Fit compares each role with your profile: a full dot is a good fit, a half-filled dot means check something (a visa, a graduation-year rule, a job for after you graduate), an empty dot is a long shot (for example a PhD-only role while you're an undergrad). The reason shows under the company; open the row for all of them.

When is a January–December strip. months in class: UNESP's semesters (Mar–Jun, Aug–Nov), or the months your résumé summary lists as free. the role runs in a break. it clashes with classes.

Résumé match compares the skills a posting mentions (Python, PyTorch, ROS, PLC…) and its area and title with the summary your AI chat made from your résumé. Strong and Good matches get a tag; open a row to see which skills match and which are missing. It's keyword matching, so read the posting before deciding.

Pay appears when the posting states it (hr = per hour, mo = per month, yr = per year). Annual figures for internships are usually the hourly rate scaled to a year.

☆ Save builds a shortlist, ✕ Hide removes a role from your lists, and inside a row you can mark it Applied. All of this stays in this browser. vagaLume never hides anything by itself: labels and fit are hints, not verdicts.

Your filters live in the page address, so you can bookmark a view (for example "ML roles in Europe") or send it to a friend.
